## About project

Filter for pycantus (as well as PyCantus library itself) is being developed under the Digital Analysis of Chant Transmission ([DACT](https://dact-chant.ca/)). This project, funded by the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada, aims to advance the study of chant dissemination using computational tools.

Explore more about the project, its goals and contacts on our [homepage](https://ufal.mff.cuni.cz/grants/dact).
